WHAT THE SYSTEM CAN CLAIM
  1. Inference Time of Untrained Architecture: 34.51 ms per tile (310.59 ms for 9 tiles); no fidelity claim (cite reports/step4_latency_summary.csv).
  2. Interactive Evacuation Re-Planning: Network router evaluates 200 origins in 2,115.74 ms (10.58 ms/origin) (cite reports/step4_latency_summary.csv).
  3. Burnover Margins by Method (Inconclusive): Negative burnovers at 60m are 1 of 177 for static, 0 for other methods; at 30m B on A2-assim median 35.9 from n=13 origins vs 84.5 for static from n=118; no CI computed; inconclusive (cite reports/step2_hazard_type_margins.csv).
  4. Burnover Timing Error in Teacher: Seed 42 is low-area seed (5,491 ha, ~5th percentile); 8 of 10 corridor burnovers never burn and 2 arrive with median +157 min late (cite reports/step2_timing_by_hazard_type.csv).
WHAT THE SYSTEM CANNOT CLAIM
  1. NO Safety Guarantees: Academic research demonstration only; never for live tactical navigation or life-safety decisions.
  2. NO Superiority in Overall Margins: A2-assim median truth margin -37.0 min vs -31.5 min for static against all 436 NIST hazards; cause not tested.
  3. NO Independent Ground Truth: Teacher field not validated against road impassability. Assimilation ingests empirical observations sharing source document with hazards.
  4. NO Production Readiness: Checkpoint from phase 3A trained 2 epochs on 66 smoke shards; A3-reignite burned 85.5% of tile; cannot be claimed as validated surrogate.
AUDITED LIMITATIONS (FROM REPORT.MD)
  • 1. HRRR Analysis vs Forecast: Atmospheric wind inputs use post-event analysis (fxx=0), not operational forecasts.
  • 2. NIST Timestamps: Hazard onsets reconstructed from NIST TN 2252; abandoned vehicles have modelled constant onset at 87.0 min (harness assumption, not observation).
  • 3. K=3 Corridors: Destination exits grouped into 3 corridors (Skyway, Clark Rd, Pentz/Hwy 70).
  • 4. Physics Calibration Drift: Teacher under-burns Camp Fire by ~2x and over-burns training fires (Mean Area Ratio 5.597).
  • 5. Development Case: Camp Fire is unseen development case, strictly excluded from surrogate training.
  • 6. Shared Provenance: Observation assimilation shares source data with evaluation hazards.
  • 7. Provider B Heuristic: Uses geometric directional cone rather than microscopic ember simulator.
  • 8. Vehicles Finish Before Fire Arrives in Physics Simulation: Evacuation vehicles complete travel before the simulated fire reaches Paradise (smallest positive predicted margin 149.8 min; cite reports/step5c_route_safety_margins.csv).
  • 9. Route Redundancy: Agents A and A2-pure produce identical routes in every table.
  • 10. Surrogate Over-Burn: Phase 4A surrogate output covered whole domain; A3-reignite burned 85.5% of tile.
  • 11. No Held-Out Teacher Test: No held-out teacher fire test was conducted.
  • 12. No Trained Surrogate Result: No trained surrogate result is reported in this version.
  • 13. Measured Latencies: 34.51 ms per tile, 310.59 ms domain coverage (retracted earlier narrative 24.1 ms).
