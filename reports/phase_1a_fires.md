# Phase 1A Step 3 — Per-Fire Calibration Setup (`reports/phase_1a_fires.md`)

**Operating Grid Resolution:** `60 m` (projected in `EPSG:32610`, UTM Zone 10N; $1\text{ cell} = 0.36\text{ ha}$)  
**Single Pre-Fire LANDFIRE Vintage:** `LANDFIRE 2016 Remap (LF 2.0.0)` (strictly predates all 2019–2021 training fires and the held-out 2018 Camp Fire; zero burn-scar leakage)  
**Satellite Progression Scales:**
- **GOFER (`GOFER_Combined`)**: Geostationary GOES-East + GOES-West hourly progression perimeters (`dt = 1.0 h`), evaluated by aggregating simulated 60 m burned cells into $33 \times 33$ blocks ($1,980\text{ m} \approx 2.0\text{ km}$ native GOES scale).
- **FEDS (`Zenodo 15261836`)**: Polar-orbiting VIIRS 375 m 12-hourly active fire observations, evaluated by aggregating simulated 60 m burned cells into $6 \times 6$ blocks ($360\text{ m} \approx 375\text{ m}$ native VIIRS scale).

## 2. GOFER Area-vs-Time Justification for Each Calibration Window

1. **Kincade (2019)**:
   - Ignited at `2019-10-24 04:00 UTC` during a severe Diablo wind event. At GOFER step 1 (`2019-10-24 05:00 UTC`), the active perimeter is $846.8\text{ ha}$ ($8.47\text{ km}^2$). Over the next 12–24 hours, the fire expands unsuppressed to $11,628.2\text{ ha}$ ($13.7\times$ growth), with active fire line spread rates reaching $4.89\text{ km/h}$, before winds temporarily abated on Oct 25 ($13,555.0\text{ ha}$ at +48 h).
2. **Bobcat (2020)**:
   - Ignited near Cogswell Dam at `2020-09-06 19:00 UTC`. From GOFER step 1 (`2020-09-06 20:00 UTC`, $350.3\text{ ha}$), the fire ran upslope into the steep San Gabriel Mountains canyons driven by topographic channeling and plume convection, growing continuously to $1,892.0\text{ ha}$ (+6 h), $3,616.2\text{ ha}$ (+12 h), $5,379.7\text{ ha}$ (+24 h), and $7,399.4\text{ ha}$ (+48 h).
3. **Glass (2020)**:
   - During the initial 14 hours (`2020-09-27 11:00` to `2020-09-28 00:00 UTC`), Glass grew moderately from $120.5\text{ ha}$ to $2,589.8\text{ ha}$. At step 15 (`2020-09-28 01:00 UTC`, $2,616.4\text{ ha}$), peak nocturnal Diablo offshore winds hit the Napa/Sonoma ridge system, driving the maximum 24-hour unsuppressed surge of $+26,361.7\text{ ha}$ ($2,616.4 \to 14,922.8\text{ ha}$ at +6 h $\to 22,820.4\text{ ha}$ at +12 h $\to 28,978.1\text{ ha}$ at +24 h, and $30,032.3\text{ ha}$ at +48 h).
4. **Creek (2020)**:
   - Steps 1–5 in GOFER had zero polygon area. After initial establishment on the morning of Sep 5, at step 13 (`2020-09-05 14:00 UTC`, $1,862.9\text{ ha}$), the fire erupted into a historic pyrocumulonimbus plume run through heavy drought-killed Sierra conifer timber, gaining $+30,185.6\text{ ha}$ in 24 hours ($8,406.7\text{ ha}$ at +6 h, $16,690.4\text{ ha}$ at +12 h, $32,048.5\text{ ha}$ at +24 h, and $48,486.4\text{ ha}$ at +48 h).
5. **Zogg (2020)**:
   - Immediately upon first detection at step 1 (`2020-09-27 22:00 UTC`, $73.7\text{ ha}$), Zogg was driven by strong N-NE downslope winds in Shasta County, exploding to $10,433.8\text{ ha}$ in 6 hours, $12,674.4\text{ ha}$ in 12 hours, $16,454.9\text{ ha}$ in 24 hours, and $21,944.6\text{ ha}$ in 48 hours ($98.5\%$ of its entire lifetime footprint).
6. **Caldor (2021)**:
   - In its first 34 hours (`2021-08-15 08:00` to `2021-08-16 18:00 UTC`), Caldor remained confined to the deep Cosumnes River gorge ($\le 1,444.3\text{ ha}$). At step 53 (`2021-08-17 06:00 UTC`, $3,958.6\text{ ha}$), strong SW winds aligned with the canyon axis, triggering Caldor's peak 24-hour unsuppressed run of $+26,335.2\text{ ha}$ ($9,199.9\text{ ha}$ at +6 h $\to 11,373.7\text{ ha}$ at +12 h $\to 30,293.8\text{ ha}$ at +24 h, and $32,168.7\text{ ha}$ at +48 h).
7. **Tamarack (2021)**:
   - Ignited by lightning on July 4, 2021 (`2021-07-04 18:00 UTC`), Tamarack smoldered at $\le 609.2\text{ ha}$ for 12 full days (GOFER steps 1–291). At step 292 (`2021-07-16 22:00 UTC`, $716.1\text{ ha}$), high-elevation SW winds and canyon alignment caused an unsuppressed breakout to $6,700.0\text{ ha}$ (+6 h), $7,544.0\text{ ha}$ (+12 h), $11,634.8\text{ ha}$ (+24 h), and $20,114.6\text{ ha}$ (+48 h).

## 3. Non-Saturating Domain Verification

Each fire's simulation grid is sized between $720 \times 720$ ($43.2\text{ km} \times 43.2\text{ km} = 186,624\text{ ha}$) and $840 \times 840$ ($50.4\text{ km} \times 50.4\text{ km} = 254,016\text{ ha}$) at 60 m resolution, centered on the 48-hour progression footprint. Across all 7 fires, the domain area is **$6.4\times$ to $34.7\times$ larger** than the observed 24-hour fire footprint, providing a buffer of $>12\text{ km}$ on all margins so the cellular automaton cannot saturate the grid.
